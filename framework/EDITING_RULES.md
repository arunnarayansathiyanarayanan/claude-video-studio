# Editing rules (approved): every edit follows these

Scope: any footage (event, headshot/talking head, interview, demo, tutorial, announcement, behind-the-scenes), short or long, for **Instagram** or **LinkedIn**.
Platform rules (section 1) + use-case rules (section 2) + length rules (section 3) stack on top of each other; universal rules (section 4) always apply. Motion details live in `MOTION_STYLE.md`; timing plans in `templates/blueprints/`.

---

## 0. Intake: asked before every edit (no editing until answered)

Asked as two quick-pick rounds (max 4 questions each): round 1 = 1, 2, 3, 5 · round 2 = 4, 6–9 only for what `creator.config.md` doesn't already answer.

| # | Question | Options |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Platform** | Instagram · LinkedIn · Both (separate cut for each, not one resized) |
| 2 | **Video type** | Event recap · Talking head / headshot · Interview / podcast clip · Product demo / screen recording · Tutorial / explainer · Announcement / launch · Behind-the-scenes / day-in-life · Other |
| 3 | **Length** | Short (Reel 7–60 s / LinkedIn 30–90 s) · Long (2–10 min) · Exact length |
| 4 | **Goal + audience** | Reach / shares · Authority / credibility · Leads / sign-ups · Community. Who should watch? |
| 5 | **References** | 1–3 videos/posts/templates + what you like in each · use my saved library · surprise me |
| 6 | **Content inputs** | Script or key points? Speaker audio usable? Any facts to cite? Must-include shots/people? |
| 7 | **Brand + text** | Fonts/colours/logo, or neutral · caption language(s) · on-screen name/title |
| 8 | **Audio** | My track · Original score · Speech only · (Instagram) add trending audio in-app later |
| 9 | **CTA** | Comment question · Follow · Link in first comment / bio · Save / share · None |

## 1. Platform rules

| Area | Instagram (casual, contemporary) | LinkedIn (formal, credible) |
|---|---|---|
| **Format** | 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps (60 if footage allows), H.264 + AAC, MP4 | 4:5 1080×1350 default · 1:1 for talking heads · 16:9 for screen recordings / long form · MP4 only (MOV fails) |
| **Safe zone** | Keep text/faces out of the top 270 px, bottom 670 px, right 230 px (action buttons) and 65 px each side. Cover must also work cropped to 3:4 (centre 1080×1440) for the profile grid | Keep text inside the centre 1080×1080 so it survives 1:1 crops; 72 px margins |
| **Length (short)** | 7–15 s for trends/teasers (best completion) · 30–60 s for tips and stories (most reach, saves and shares) · avoid 60–90 s unless your data supports it | 30–90 s default · 15–30 s quick tip · 2–5 min tutorial / interview · up to 10 min deep dive |
| **Hook** | Payoff or the most surprising frame first; on-screen text by 1 s; no logo intro; ask a question early and answer it later | One clear statement or result in the first 2–3 s; the first frame must make sense with sound off; never a slow title card |
| **Pacing** | Fast: new visual every 2–4 s, jump cuts, punch-in zooms (110–125%), beat-synced cuts; something new every few seconds | Measured: 2–4 s shots, breathing room after key points, cuts on sentence ends, not beats |
| **Captions on screen** | Big, bold, centred word-by-word captions (2–4 words at a time) with the key word highlighted in the accent colour; casual phrasing and an occasional emoji are fine | Clean sentence captions (1–2 lines, max ~42 characters per line) in the lower third; upload an SRT too (auto-captions mangle jargon and names); no emojis |
| **Typography** | Expressive: heavier weights, playful scale changes, handwritten accents allowed | Restrained: one grotesk, two weights, no novelty fonts |
| **Motion** | Energetic within MOTION_STYLE.md: snappy springs, whip / stretch transitions, kinetic text, stickers and arrows, loop-friendly ending | Calm within MOTION_STYLE.md: masked reveals, card moves, data callouts, lower thirds; max one "signature" motion moment |
| **Colour / grade** | Vivid, warm, punchy contrast; native phone look is fine (polished-but-not-ad) | Natural, accurate skin tones, slightly muted; brand-consistent |
| **Audio** | Trending audio performs best within 24–48 h of trending. Because it's licensed, it can't be baked in: export a version with speech + SFX only and add the trend sound in the Instagram app. Otherwise original audio/score. Sound design (whooshes, pops) welcome | Speech first; quiet music bed (−20 to −24 dB under voice) or none; minimal SFX; never trending sounds |
| **Copy tone** | Casual, first person, short lines, playful | Professional, insight-led, specific, sourced; first person but no slang |
| **Ending** | Seamless loop back to the hook, or a quick text CTA (follow / save / send to a friend) | An engagement question card + name/title lockup; links go in the first comment |
| **Cover / thumbnail** | Bold 2–4 word title, face if possible, readable when cropped to 3:4 | Simple editorial: photo, one headline, one accent word (existing simple template) |
| **Post caption** | Hook line, 1–3 short lines, CTA, 3–5 relevant hashtags | Standalone hook line, then numbered insights, question, 4–5 hashtags, sources in the first comment |
| **Algorithm focus** | Completion, replays, shares/sends, saves | Completion rate / watch time, comments in the first hour, profile visits |

## 2. Use-case rules (apply on top of the platform rules)

| Use case | Structure | Editing rules | Instagram twist | LinkedIn twist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Event recap** | Hook → recap montage → (explainer) → takeaways → outro | Best moment first; photos as cards; caption every speaker by name only if visible or verified; consent for people on camera | 15–30 s vibe reel, beat-synced, "POV: you're at X" or "3 things from X" | 30–60 s, sourced takeaways and a "my lens" box (current blueprint) |
| **Talking head / headshot** | Hook line → 3–5 points → payoff → CTA | Transcribe; cut filler, silences > 0.4 s and retakes; punch-in zoom every 1–2 sentences; B-roll or graphics for each point; eye-line stays in the top third; light grade + skin-tone check | Jump cuts, word-by-word captions, emphasis zooms, captions animated in on the beat | 1:1 or 4:5, sentence captions, name/title lower third in the first 5 s, gentler zooms (105–110%) |
| **Interview / podcast clip** | Strongest quote first → context → answer → CTA to the full episode | Pick 1 self-contained idea; two-shot ↔ single cuts on speaker change; speaker name lower thirds | Quote as the hook text; 30–60 s | Quote card + guest credentials; 60–120 s |
| **Product demo / screen recording** | Problem → "watch this" → steps → result | Zoom and pan to the area being used; cursor highlight; speed up waiting parts (show it's sped up); callouts for each step | 9:16 crop with a tight zoom; 20–45 s; result first | 16:9 or 4:5 with framed UI; 30–90 s; benefit-led captions |
| **Tutorial / explainer** | Promise → numbered steps → recap | One idea per step; numbered on-screen list; flat UI or whiteboard scenes; recap card | 30–45 s, "save this" CTA | 1–3 min, chapters for long cuts, sources on screen |
| **Announcement / launch** | Headline → what's new → why it matters → how to get it | Big headline frame; product visuals; one stat; clear CTA | Teaser energy, countdown or reveal, 10–20 s | Formal and benefit-led, 30–60 s, link in first comment |
| **Behind the scenes / day-in-life** | Time-stamped moments → reflection | Natural sound; timestamps as stickers; lo-fi is fine | Native feel, trending format, 15–45 s | Framed as a lesson or "what I learned", 45–90 s |

## 3. Short vs long format

| Area | Short (≤ 90 s) | Long (2–10 min) |
|---|---|---|
| Density | One idea, every second earns its place | Chapters (title cards or lower-third chapter tags); a recap at the end |
| Text | Heavy on-screen text and captions | Captions + key-point callouts only; less motion per minute |
| Pacing | 1–3 s shots | 3–8 s shots; rest beats between chapters |
| Format | 9:16 (Instagram) / 4:5 (LinkedIn) | LinkedIn 16:9 or 1:1; Instagram long Reels only for existing followers (Reels > 3 min aren't recommended to new audiences) |
| Extras | Loop / CTA | Intro promise in the first 10 s, chapters, an end card with next steps |

## 4. Universal rules (every video)

1. **Intake first, reference first:** no editing before section 0 is answered and references are analysed (`ref_analyze.py` + style brief).
2. **Style gate:** style frames + a 5 s test beside the reference; build after your yes.
3. **Truth:** only facts visible in the footage, confirmed by you, or cited; no invented quotes.
4. **Sound-off proof:** every video must make sense muted (captions, on-screen text).
5. **Safe zones and spelling** checked on every cut; names spelled as shown on screen.
6. **Audio:** −14 LUFS for Instagram / LinkedIn, speech always intelligible; Claude can't listen, so you judge the mix.
7. **Consent and rights:** only post footage you may share; no copyrighted music baked in; references inspire, never copied.
8. **Deliverables per platform:** video (+ a no-music version for Instagram), cover/thumbnail, post caption, SRT captions for talking-head videos.
9. **Learn:** every comment you make becomes a dated rule in LEARNINGS.md.

| Reference matching | `ref_analyze.py` + `style_brief.md` |
| Timing per use case × platform | `templates/blueprints/BLUEPRINTS.md` + `blueprints.json` |
| Covers / thumbnails | `templates/thumbnail/thumb.html` (LinkedIn) · `cover_ig.html` (Instagram 9:16 with 3:4 crop guide) |
| Post captions | `templates/project/caption.md` (LinkedIn) · `caption_instagram.md` |
