# Blueprints: default timing per use case × platform

Starting points, not laws: match the reference's measured pace (`style_dna.md`) and the creator's answers. Times in seconds.

## Event recap
| Instagram Reel (20 s) | LinkedIn (60 s) |
|---|---|
| 0–1.5 hook: best moment + 2–4 word text · 1.5–15 beat-synced montage (1–2 s shots), "POV/3 things" captions · 15–19 one-line takeaway · 19–20 loop back to the hook frame | 0–2.5 hook · 2.5–14 recap (cards, "that's me") · 14–38.5 explainer (4 beats) · 38.5–53.3 three sourced takeaways · 53.3–60 outro + question |

## Talking head / headshot
| Instagram Reel (30–45 s) | LinkedIn (45–90 s, 1:1 or 4:5) |
|---|---|
| 0–2 hook line (big caption) · then 3–5 points, punch-in every 1–2 sentences, B-roll/graphic per point · last 3 s CTA + loop | 0–3 statement + name/title lower third · points with sentence captions, gentle zooms (105–110%) · last 5 s question card |

## Interview / podcast clip
| Instagram (30–60 s) | LinkedIn (60–120 s) |
|---|---|
| Strongest quote first (as hook text) → context → answer → "full episode" CTA | Quote card + guest credentials → answer → takeaway → link in first comment |

## Product demo / screen recording
| Instagram (20–45 s, 9:16 tight zoom) | LinkedIn (30–90 s, 16:9 or 4:5) |
|---|---|
| Result first (1–2 s) → steps with zoom/pan + cursor highlight → result again → CTA | Problem (3 s) → steps with callouts → benefit → CTA |

## Tutorial / explainer
| Instagram (30–45 s) | LinkedIn (1–3 min) |
|---|---|
| Promise → numbered steps (1 idea each) → recap → "save this" | Promise → chapters with title tags → sources on screen → recap card |

## Announcement / launch
| Instagram (10–20 s) | LinkedIn (30–60 s) |
|---|---|
| Teaser/reveal or countdown → product hero → date/CTA | Headline → what's new → why it matters (one stat) → how to get it |

## Behind the scenes / day-in-life
| Instagram (15–45 s) | LinkedIn (45–90 s) |
|---|---|
| Timestamp stickers on natural moments, trending format, lo-fi OK | "What I learned" framing, 3 moments → lesson |

## Long form (2–10 min, any type)
Intro promise in the first 10 s → chapters (title cards / lower-third chapter tags) every 60–120 s → recap → end card. 3–8 s shots, captions + key-point callouts only.
